Tap to Breathe Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Tap to Breathe is a simple, offline mindfulness companion. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your data.

Summary

Tap to Breathe does not collect, store on any server, or transmit any personal information whatsoever. All session data lives only on your device.

What the app does

The app provides three guided breathing patterns (box breathing, 4-7-8 relaxation, and resonance breathing). When you complete a session, the app records the time of the session, which pattern you used, the duration, and how many cycles you completed. This record is stored locally on your device using the iOS standard UserDefaults / Android SharedPreferences mechanism.

What the app does not do

  • It does not require you to create an account.
  • It does not collect your name, email, location, or device identifiers.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • It does not contact any server during normal use.
  • It does not share data with third parties, because there is no data to share.

Your control over your data

You can delete all locally stored data at any time from the app's "More" page (coming soon) or by deleting and reinstalling the app.

Children's privacy

Tap to Breathe is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no information from anyone, we collect no information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
